Panda Blink, in the look you picked

A 3D panda face with only eyes and nose, and the app restyled after your three references: the black hero card and floating tab bar, soft rounded cards with a round icon rail, and clean folder cards with an info panel.

Mascot

A panda face, nothing else

A soft white 3D face with two glossy black eye patches, glowing eyes inside them, and a small shiny nose. No ears, no mouth. The eyes still blink and still carry the mood colour.

  • The patches tilt outward like a real panda; the eyes inside keep the slight tilt from round 1.
  • Mood shows in the eyes only: colour and shape. The face never changes, so it stays calm.
  • In the notch the black hardware is the face, so the patches become dark glass with the eyes and a glossy nose bump inside.
