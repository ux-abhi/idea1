Overview

What Blink is

1 · Part of your lifeComes to you: waking, laptop open, before and after meetings, at night.
2 · Prepares aheadPlans tomorrow with you; drafts and researches overnight.
3 · Asks firstEvery action is a card. Send, post and money need Face ID.
4 · Everything linkedMeetings, notes, mail, people, tasks, segments, money, content.
5 · Claude is the brainAuto picks Opus, Sonnet or Haiku per job. Switch any time.
6 · Ask onceOne setup chat, then it learns from yes, edit and skip.
7 · Private and lightYour server, your keys. The server works; devices sleep.
Mascot · current

Glossy bot, panda duo-tone eyes

  • White glossy 3D shell, black glass eye patches, light eyes. No nose, no mouth.
  • Mood shows in the eyes only: white rest, teal heads-up, coral important, blue thinking, green done (∩∩), amber sleepy, closed in focus.
  • Correction: on the Mac the eyes can’t sit inside the camera cutout (no pixels there). They live in the left ear beside it, and under it when the notch opens.
  • Rive for the app and notch; pre-rendered still poses for iPhone surfaces.
Mac notch · true geometry

Built around the camera, not on it

The notch is a hole in the display. Blink draws a black shape that is wider than the hardware, so the two blend into one notch. Small content goes in the ears left and right of the camera at menu-bar height; bigger content opens below the menu-bar line as a wide, short panel.

Architecture

How it fits together

Your devices
Mac app + notch (SwiftUI, AppKit, DynamicNotchKit)iPhone app (widgets, Live Activities, App Intents)Shortcuts: Apple Pay, bank textsClaude app (connector)
Your server
Supabase: Postgres + vectors, Edge Functions, cron, VaultConnections gateway (MCP)Mail worker (IMAP/SMTP)Model router + cost meterContent engine + playbooks
Outside
Claude API (your key)Google · Instagram · LinkedIn · XPexilim push relay (encrypted only)Apple Push
  • Pexilim runs no database. Keys and data stay on each person’s server; push content is end-to-end encrypted.
  • Claude tools can only propose actions; nothing happens without approval. 🔴 always needs Face ID.
  • Shipped code is MIT/Apache/BSD only. 64 Claude skills help development and never ship.
