Rules

How it behaves

  • Creating a segment and its reminders takes one tap. Emailing a client or posting still needs Face ID.
  • Blink suggests a segment once per topic. If you say no, it remembers.
  • Templates are data, so new ones arrive without an app update. A segment you like can become your own template.
  • On Done, Blink writes a short retro: what shipped, what slipped, time spent.
  • Later: share a segment with family, a client or a teammate. Data stays on the owner’s server.
