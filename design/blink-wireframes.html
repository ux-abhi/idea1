Bot design

One character, three bodies

From your references: the glossy white helmet with a black glass visor and glowing oval eyes, and the soft blue glass bubble. The big idea: the visor and the notch are the same thing. On the Mac the notch is Blink’s visor, so the hardware becomes the face.

Visor Blink (main)White shell, black glass visor, warm glowing eyes. For the app icon, splash, setup and the Blink panel.
Bubble Blink (soft mode)Glass blob from your blue reference. For widgets, empty states and a calmer theme.
Notch BlinkThe visor only. Eyes and glow stay inside the notch outline; eye colour carries the mood.

Bubble
  • Kept from round 1: the slight eye tilt (so it never reads as a pause icon), the blink, the mood colours, and the rule that glow stays inside the notch.
  • New: real 3D materials. Glossy shell, glass visor with a reflection, and eyes that glow like light behind glass. Built in Rive for the app, still renders for iPhone surfaces.
  • Eye colour = mood: warm yellow at rest, teal for time, coral for important, green for done.

Libraries and references

  • DynamicNotchKit (MIT): the base. Shows SwiftUI content from the notch, with expanded and compact states.
  • TasksInNotch: a small open-source notch task app built on DynamicNotchKit. Reference for structure.
  • DynamicNotch and Boring Notch: ideas only (check licences before reading code).
  • Apple Design Resources: official Figma and Sketch kits for macOS and iOS, including Dynamic Island templates, for mock-ups.
