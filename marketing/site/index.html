Set up in three minutes.

No code, no terminal.

  1. Download and open Blink.The first time, macOS asks you to confirm in System Settings. The install guide shows how.
  2. Sign in with Supabase.Blink creates your free server and installs everything on it. Already have one? Paste its address instead.
  3. Add a free key and your calendar.Paste a free Groq key and allow Calendar. Your next meeting shows up in the notch.

Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. Blink is free to download and use. You only pay for services you choose to add, like a Claude key.

Do I need to code?

No. Setup is a few clicks: sign in with Supabase and Blink creates and sets up your server for you. If you already run a Supabase server, you can paste its address instead.

What does it cost to run?

Nothing, if you want. Free models on Groq handle everyday work, and Supabase's free tier holds your data. Claude is optional, with your own Anthropic key; paid models stop for the day when they reach your daily limit ($5 unless you change it). If you outgrow Supabase's free tier, their paid plan is billed to you by Supabase.

Which Macs does it run on?

MacBooks with a notch, running macOS 26 or later.

Is my data safe?

Your data lives on your own server, in your own Supabase account, and AI requests use your own keys. Pexilim runs no database and can't read your data. Your clipboard never leaves your Mac. Blink never sends, posts or spends without your OK, and the important actions need Touch ID. See the privacy policy for the details.

Is there an iPhone app?

Later. An iPhone app with the Dynamic Island is planned; Blink is Mac first.
